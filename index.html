HOW TO PLAY

Move

Use arrow keys or WASD to steer through the maze. On mobile, use the on-screen D-pad or swipe.

Eat Pellets

Collect every pellet to clear the level. Each one adds to your score — clean the maze to advance.

Avoid Ghosts

Four ghosts hunt you through the maze. One touch costs a life — unless they're vulnerable.

Power Up

Grab a power pellet to turn the tables — ghosts turn vulnerable and can be eaten for bonus points.
